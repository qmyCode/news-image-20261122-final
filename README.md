Sunday 22 November 2026: 38 dated stories, 7 regions, one date check. One page summing up the day 186 drones came over Ukraine in the night, COP31 ran 44 hours into overtime in Antalya and still agreed nothing, Iran claimed two US radars in Bahrain while Bahrain denied it, and India declared on a record 720 for 9 in Wellington — three large illustrated scenes, twelve more illustrated story cards, a world map with numbered pins, a six-number stat bar and a region tally, 23 further briefs and results, every story verified against wire reporting and date-checked on the page itself (the DATE CHECK band names the famous stories that were *not* Sunday's and were left out).

The image is drawn by `draw.py` (a build tool, Pillow) and committed as `entry.png`; `main.py` is a stdlib-only copier that verifies and copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
